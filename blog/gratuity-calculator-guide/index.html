How to use a gratuity estimate carefully and why employee category and applicable rules matter.

A gratuity calculator is an estimation aid: it translates salary and qualifying service inputs into an indicative amount. The important part is understanding which salary figure and service period the calculation is using before treating the result as a planning number.

When a different workflow is better

Use the result for planning and checking arithmetic, not as a substitute for an official settlement statement. FAQ

Is the calculator an official gratuity sanction?

No. It is an estimation tool; the final amount depends on the applicable employment rules and official records.

Which salary should I enter?

Use the salary component requested by the calculator and the applicable gratuity rules for your case rather than automatically entering gross salary.

Does every service period count in the same way?

Not necessarily. Qualifying-service rules and rounding can affect the result.

Why should I save my inputs?

Keeping the assumptions makes it easier to explain or reproduce the estimate later.
