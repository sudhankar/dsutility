How to Calculate DA Arrears: A Practical Guide for Employees

DA arrears arise when the applicable Dearness Allowance rate changes but the revised amount is paid later. A useful calculator should separate the rate assumptions from the monthly amounts so the difference can be checked month by month.

This guide focuses on how to calculate da arrears: a practical guide for employees from a practical user perspective: what to prepare, what to check during the operation, and what can go wrong before you share the final result.

FAQ

Why does the detailed table show amounts instead of percentages?

The percentage is the rate used for calculation; Due, Drawn and Difference are the resulting monthly money amounts.

Can Drawn be different every month?

Yes. The optional month-wise mode is intended for departments where actual drawn amounts vary by month.

How is Difference calculated?

For the detailed workflow it is the Due amount minus the Drawn amount for the corresponding month.

Is the calculator an official DA arrear statement?

No. It helps calculate and organize the figures; the applicable order and payroll records remain authoritative.
