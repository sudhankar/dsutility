FAQ

Does the calculator replace an official GPF statement?

No. It is a calculation aid and the official statement remains authoritative.

Why can the interest amount differ from my statement?

The statement may use period-specific rates, dates, rounding or adjustments not represented by the simple inputs.

What should I check first when the closing balance looks wrong?

Verify the opening balance, contribution amounts, interest rate and period.

Can I use it for several financial periods?

Yes if the tool supports the required period inputs, but verify each period before carrying the balance forward.
