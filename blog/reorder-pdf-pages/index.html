FAQ

Can I reorder pages without changing their content?

Yes. Reordering changes the page sequence rather than rewriting the page artwork.

Why should I check section boundaries?

A visually similar page can be placed in the wrong section even when the thumbnails look correct.

Will printed page numbers update?

No. They are part of the existing page content.

Should I keep the original?

Yes, until the new sequence has been verified.
