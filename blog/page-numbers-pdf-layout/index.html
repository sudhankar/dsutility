How to Add Page Numbers to a PDF: Positions, Formats and Margins

Page numbers are simple visually but easy to get wrong when a document has covers, front matter or mixed page sizes. Decide what counts as page one before applying a numbering style.

This guide focuses on how to add page numbers to a pdf: positions, formats and margins from a practical user perspective: what to prepare, what to check during the operation, and what can go wrong before you share the final result.

FAQ

Can I start numbering after the cover?

That depends on the tool’s page-range controls; select the pages that should receive numbers.

Why do numbers overlap my footer?

The chosen position or margin may be too close to existing content.

Do page numbers change the PDF page count?

No. They add visible content to existing pages.

Should I number every page?

Not necessarily. Follow the document’s intended structure and submission requirements.
