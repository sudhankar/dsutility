About PPF Calculator

This PPF calculator models one planned annual contribution in an Apr–Mar financial-year sequence. Interest is estimated using the scheme rule that the lowest balance between the close of the fifth day and month-end is eligible for that month. It is designed for planning and comparison, not as an official account statement.

The current default rate is 7.1% because the National Savings Institute currently lists PPF at 7.1% for the 2026–27 quarterly rate table. Rates can change, so the calculator keeps the rate editable.

How to use PPF Calculator

  1. Enter the opening PPF balance if you already have one.
  2. Enter the planned contribution for each financial year; the calculator caps the default field at ₹1,50,000 for the standard annual contribution limit.
  3. Choose the contribution month and deposit day. Deposits made on or before the 5th are treated as earning that month in this model.
  4. Set the number of years and review contributions, estimated interest and maturity value separately.
  5. Change one assumption at a time to compare scenarios.

Technical limitations

  • The model uses one planned annual contribution rather than every possible deposit pattern.
  • It does not reproduce an official PPF passbook or account-office calculation.
  • Actual interest rules, extensions, withdrawals, loans and irregular deposits can change the outcome.
  • Rates for future periods can change. Check the current official rate and scheme rules separately; this tool provides a mathematical estimate only.

Frequently asked

What PPF interest rate is used by default?

The calculator starts at 7.1%, matching the current National Savings Institute rate table, but the rate is editable because government rates can change.

Why does the deposit day matter?

PPF interest is linked to the lowest balance between the close of the fifth day and the end of the month. This calculator therefore asks for a contribution day and uses that timing in its estimate.

Does the calculator guarantee my PPF maturity amount?

No. It is an estimate based on your inputs and the selected rate. Future notified rates and your actual deposit pattern can change the result.

Is this an official PPF statement?

No. It is a planning calculator. Use your official account statement and the applicable scheme rules for an authoritative balance.

Calculation basis

The estimate follows the PPF rule that interest is based on the lowest eligible monthly balance and is credited annually. For current scheme details, see the SBI PPF information page and the India Post savings-schemes page. These external references are authoritative sources for current rules and rates; DSUTILITY provides only a mathematical estimate.
