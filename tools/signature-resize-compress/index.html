Frequently asked questions

Does auto-trim change the handwriting?

No. It crops surrounding blank space; it does not redraw the signature.

Can I set a maximum KB?

Yes. The encoder adjusts quality and, when necessary, scales the output down to approach the selected maximum.

Should I use the exact portal dimensions?

Yes. Enter the current requirement shown by the application portal or notice rather than relying on a generic preset.

Is my signature uploaded?

For this supported workflow, processing happens in your browser and the original file remains on your device.
