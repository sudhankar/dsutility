About Enhance PDF

Enhance PDF improves the visual presentation of supported PDF pages by adjusting scan-oriented properties such as contrast, brightness, color, and cleanup controls. It is most useful for scanned documents that are readable but look faded, gray, or uneven.

Good practice: Before creating the final enhance pdf result, keep the original source available and decide what the output must achieve. A quick review of page order, dimensions, readability, or calculation inputs can prevent a technically correct file from being unsuitable for its actual purpose.

Before you finish: Treat the preview as a review stage, not just a download step. Check the parts that matter most for enhance pdf, then open the exported file or result once more before sharing, printing, submitting, or replacing the original.

How to use Enhance PDF

  1. Upload the PDF
  2. inspect the first-page preview
  3. adjust enhancement controls gradually
  4. compare the preview with the original
  5. apply the settings
  6. review several pages
  7. export the enhanced PDF.

Technical limitations

  • Enhancement cannot recreate detail that was never captured by the scanner or camera.
  • Aggressive contrast or sharpening can make noise and compression artifacts more visible.
  • Different pages may need different settings, while a single setting can affect all selected pages.
  • Enhancement is not OCR; use OCR PDF when searchable text is required.

Frequently asked

Can enhancement make a blurry scan sharp?

It can improve contrast and perceived clarity, but it cannot recover missing detail.

Should I increase contrast fully?

No. Increase controls gradually and compare against the original.

Can I enhance color documents?

Use the color controls available, but verify that important colors remain accurate.

Does enhancement create searchable text?

No. Use OCR separately when text recognition is needed.
