About DA Arrears Calculator

DA Arrears Calculator estimates month-wise Dearness Allowance arrears from a selected period and rate change. It is designed for office-style statements where Due DA, Drawn DA, Difference, deductions, and final arrear amounts need to be reviewed in one place.

Good practice: Before creating the final da arrears calculator result, keep the original source available and decide what the output must achieve. A quick review of page order, dimensions, readability, or calculation inputs can prevent a technically correct file from being unsuitable for its actual purpose.

Before you finish: Treat the preview as a review stage, not just a download step. Check the parts that matter most for da arrears calculator, then open the exported file or result once more before sharing, printing, submitting, or replacing the original.

How to use DA Arrears Calculator

  1. Choose the From and To months
  2. enter the relevant basic pay and DA rates
  3. set the drawn rate where applicable
  4. review month-wise Due, Drawn, and Difference amounts
  5. configure optional deduction handling
  6. add employee details if required
  7. calculate
  8. review the statement
  9. print or save the landscape A4 output.

Technical limitations

  • The result is an estimate based on the values entered; departmental rules may differ.
  • DA rate effective dates and pay changes must be entered correctly for the period being calculated.
  • Deduction treatment such as GPF, NPS, UPS, or other recoveries should be checked against the applicable office rules.
  • The calculator does not verify government orders or payroll records automatically.

Frequently asked

Can Due and Drawn show amounts?

Yes. The month-wise optional columns are intended to show calculated Due Amount, Drawn Amount, and the resulting Difference; the percentages are set in the inputs above.

Can I use different rates for different months?

Yes. Use the month-wise rate structure provided by the calculator when the arrear period contains more than one rate.

Can I include deductions?

Yes, where the calculator provides GPF, NPS, UPS, or other deduction controls.

Is the output an official payroll statement?

It is a calculation aid. Verify the result against the applicable department rules and records before official use.
