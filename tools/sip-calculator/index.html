What this SIP calculator estimates

A Systematic Investment Plan calculator shows how a fixed monthly contribution could grow under an assumed annual return. It is a mathematical projection, not a promise of market performance.

Calculation method

The annual assumption is converted to an effective monthly rate so that twelve monthly periods correspond to the entered annual rate.

i = (1 + annual rate)^(1/12) − 1
FV = P × [((1+i)^n − 1) / i] × (1+i)

How to use it

  1. Enter the monthly amount.
  2. Enter an assumed annual return.
  3. Enter the number of years.
  4. Review projected value, amount invested and estimated gain.

Important limitations

  • Mutual-fund returns are market-linked and not guaranteed.
  • Taxes, expense ratios and exit loads are not modelled.
  • Actual cash flows can differ from a constant-return projection.

FAQ

Does this guarantee my SIP return?

No. The return is only an assumption for planning.

Why use an effective monthly rate?

It reflects the monthly equivalent of the entered annual compounded assumption.
