What this calculator covers

CALENDAR ACCURACY

Leap years and month lengths

Uses real calendar dates, including 29 February and 28/29/30/31-day months, rather than assuming every month has 30 days.

FLEXIBLE RESULTS

Choose the unit you need

Show years-months-days, total days, total calendar months, years, weeks or combined month/day views. Totals are calculated for the selected display.

PRACTICAL USE

Deadlines and date planning

Use quick ranges, inclusive counting, date swapping, date addition/subtraction, copy and print. Verify official deadlines and eligibility rules against the issuing authority.
