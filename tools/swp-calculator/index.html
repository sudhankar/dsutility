What an SWP calculator does

An SWP calculator models regular withdrawals from an invested lump-sum corpus. Each withdrawal reduces the balance and the remaining balance continues under the assumed return.

Calculation method

This calculator uses a monthly nominal rate and applies each scheduled withdrawal before that month's assumed growth.

B = B − W
B = B × (1 + annual rate/12)

Limitations

  • Actual market returns fluctuate.
  • Taxes, fees and transaction dates are not included.
  • If the corpus reaches zero, the model stops further withdrawals.

FAQ

Is SWP income guaranteed?

No. The withdrawal schedule is fixed in the model, but investment value can change.

Why can calculators differ?

They may use different return-rate conventions, withdrawal timing and rounding.
