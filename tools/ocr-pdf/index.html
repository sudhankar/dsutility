      About OCR PDF
     

     

      OCR PDF recognizes text from scanned or image-based PDF pages and creates a searchable text layer or extracted text result, depending on the tool workflow. It is useful for scanned forms, old records, photographed documents, and PDFs where normal text extraction returns little or nothing.
     

     

      
       Good practice:
      
      Before creating the final ocr pdf result, keep the original source available and decide what the output must achieve. A quick review of page order, dimensions, readability, or calculation inputs can prevent a technically correct file from being unsuitable for its actual purpose.
     

    

    

     

      
       Before you finish:
      
      Treat the preview as a review stage, not just a download step. Check the parts that matter most for ocr pdf, then open the exported file or result once more before sharing, printing, submitting, or replacing the original.

      Technical limitations
     

     
    
      
  • 
       OCR is probabilistic and can misread characters, numbers, punctuation, or table structure.
      
    • 
      
  • 
       Low-resolution, skewed, noisy, or handwritten pages are harder to recognize.
      
    • 
      
  • 
       OCR can require significant browser CPU and memory for long PDFs.
      
    • 
      
  • 
       Critical legal, financial, medical, or identity information should be verified against the original scan.

      Frequently asked
     

     

      

       
        Why does OCR make mistakes?
       
       

        Recognition depends on image quality, font, contrast, skew, and language.
       

      

      

       
        Can OCR make a scanned PDF searchable?
       
       

        Yes, that is one of its main purposes when the export workflow adds a text layer.
       

      

      

       
        Can it recognize tables?
       
       

        It may recognize the text, but exact table structure is not guaranteed.
       

      

      

       
        Should I verify OCR output?
       
       

        Yes. Always compare critical names, numbers, dates, and amounts with the source scan.

      Privacy
     

     

      OCR processing is performed in the browser for the supported workflow. The source PDF does not need to be uploaded to a DSUTILITY OCR server. OCR can process sensitive document content, so use a trusted device and verify the generated result before sharing.
