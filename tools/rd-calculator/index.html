About RD Calculator

RD Calculator estimates the maturity value of a recurring deposit using regular contributions, an interest-rate assumption, tenure, and the available compounding method. It is useful for comparing savings plans where money is deposited periodically rather than as one initial lump sum.

How to use RD Calculator

  1. Enter the recurring deposit amount
  2. set the interest rate
  3. choose the tenure and compounding settings
  4. calculate
  5. review total invested, estimated interest, and maturity value
  6. compare another scenario if needed.

Technical limitations

  • Banks can calculate recurring deposits using institution-specific conventions.
  • Actual maturity may differ because of rate changes, rounding, deposit dates, or special terms.
  • The calculator is not connected to a bank’s live rate or account system.
  • Tax, premature closure, and other bank-specific charges may need separate checking.

Frequently asked

Why is RD different from FD?

RD builds the deposit through repeated contributions, so each instalment can earn interest for a different period.

What does total invested mean?

It represents the sum of the recurring contributions entered for the selected tenure.

Can I change the interest rate?

Yes. Enter the rate applicable to the scenario you want to estimate.

Is the maturity value official?

No. Confirm the final amount with the bank’s product terms.
