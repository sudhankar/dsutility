Why RD calculations are different

A recurring deposit receives a new contribution every month, so each instalment earns interest for a different period.

Calculation method

M = Σ P × (1 + r/400)^(months remaining ÷ 3)

Each monthly instalment is treated separately. The quarterly rate is applied for the time that instalment remains in the account, so earlier instalments earn for longer than later ones.

Important limitations

  • Actual bank products can have different rules and rounding.
  • TDS is not deducted.
  • Late instalments and penalties are not modelled.

FAQ

Why is quarterly compounding used?

Many Indian RD products use quarterly compounding, but exact product terms should be checked.

Does it include TDS?

No. TDS depends on applicable rules and circumstances.
