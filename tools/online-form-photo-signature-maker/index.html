Before you submit
✓ Check the application instructions for exact pixels, dimensions and KB limits.
✓ Open both downloaded files and confirm they are readable at normal zoom.
✓ A successful download does not mean the receiving portal will accept every format.

Presets are practical starting points, not a guarantee of acceptance by a particular exam, employer, bank or government portal. Requirements can change.

How to use this form photo and signature maker

  1. Choose the applicant photo and select the closest preset or enter your required dimensions.
  2. Adjust the crop focus so the face and framing are appropriate for the form instructions.
  3. Choose a target KB value when the portal gives a maximum file size.
  4. Upload the signature, trim unnecessary blank margins if needed, and choose its target size.
  5. Download the two files separately and verify their actual file sizes before uploading them to the application.

Important limits

This tool can resize, crop, compress and prepare images, but it cannot know whether a particular application portal's current specification is correct. If an authority requires a particular DPI, background, file extension, signature stroke style or exact dimensions, use those instructions rather than relying on a generic preset.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a photo smaller than 50 KB?

Yes. Select a lower target such as 20 KB or 30 KB. Very small targets can require lower JPEG quality or smaller dimensions, so inspect the result before submitting it.

Can I resize the signature and remove empty margins?

Yes. The signature workflow can trim light blank margins around the source and then render it to the selected dimensions.

Does this tool upload my photo or signature?

The image processing in this workflow runs in your browser. The files do not need to be sent to a DSUTILITY processing server.

Will the 35 × 45 preset be accepted everywhere?

No. It is a practical portrait starting point. Always follow the current instructions of the specific application or portal.
