How gratuity is commonly estimated

For employees covered by the Payment of Gratuity Act, a common statutory estimate uses 15 days of wages for each completed year of qualifying service, with the daily wage convention based on 26 days. Government employees and categories governed by separate service rules can have different formulas, qualifying-service rules and ceilings.

Estimated gratuity = (Basic Pay + DA) × 15 ÷ 26 × qualifying years

This calculator treats six or more extra months as one additional qualifying year for the general estimate. Do not assume that rounding rule applies to every service category.

Before using the result officially

  • Check the rule applicable to the employee.
  • Confirm the wage definition.
  • Confirm qualifying service.
  • Confirm the current ceiling.
  • Check departmental or retirement orders.

FAQ

Is this an official government calculation?

No. It is a planning estimate and should be checked against the applicable service rules.

Why are Basic Pay and DA separate?

The general estimate may use wages that include Basic Pay and DA, but the exact definition depends on the applicable rule.

Is this an official government gratuity calculation?
