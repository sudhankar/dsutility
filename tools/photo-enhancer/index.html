About Photo Enhancer

Photo Enhancer improves a photo through adjustable browser-side controls such as brightness, contrast, sharpness, saturation, or related visual settings. It is intended for practical cleanup rather than professional raw-photo processing.

Good practice: Before creating the final photo enhancer result, keep the original source available and decide what the output must achieve. A quick review of page order, dimensions, readability, or calculation inputs can prevent a technically correct file from being unsuitable for its actual purpose.

Before you finish: Treat the preview as a review stage, not just a download step. Check the parts that matter most for photo enhancer, then open the exported file or result once more before sharing, printing, submitting, or replacing the original.

How to use Photo Enhancer

  1. Upload the original photo
  2. inspect the preview
  3. adjust one control at a time
  4. compare against the original
  5. use Reset/Revert when needed
  6. apply the final settings
  7. download the enhanced image and keep the original.

Technical limitations

  • Enhancement cannot restore detail that is absent from the source.
  • Too much sharpening can create halos or noise.
  • Colour adjustments can make skin tones or document colours inaccurate if pushed too far.
  • Browser processing is not equivalent to professional RAW development or AI restoration.

Frequently asked

Can I undo changes?

Use the Reset/Revert control to return to the original before trying a different adjustment.

Should I maximize sharpness?

No. Increase it gradually and compare fine edges with the original.

Can enhancement fix blur?

It may improve perceived contrast, but severe motion or focus blur cannot be fully recovered.

Can I use the enhanced photo for ID documents?

Check the receiving authority’s rules; excessive editing may be inappropriate for identity photographs.
