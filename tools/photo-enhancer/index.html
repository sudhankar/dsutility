Photo Enhancer

Improve brightness and contrast in a photo or scan directly in your browser.

Choose a photo to begin.

Enhancement controls

Auto Enhance gives a gentle local tone adjustment. Brightness, contrast and saturation let you tune the image. Grayscale removes colour, while Black & White creates a high-contrast monochrome result.

The current lightweight version does not claim AI super-resolution. Sharpen is reserved as a control for future stronger local sharpening, so it does not pretend to recover missing detail.

How to use it

  1. Select a photo.
  2. Try Auto Enhance first.
  3. Adjust brightness, contrast and saturation.
  4. Use Grayscale or Black & White when appropriate.
  5. Review the preview and download.

FAQ

Does Auto Enhance upload my photo?

No. The current adjustments run locally in the browser.

Can I make a photo black and white?

Yes. Enable Black & White and review the live preview.

Can enhancement restore a very blurry photo?

No. It can improve tone and contrast but cannot reliably reconstruct detail that was never captured.

Is the processing online?

The current adjustment workflow runs locally in the browser.

Photo Enhancer Guide

When brightness and contrast help, and when they cannot fix a poor source image.

What a browser photo enhancer can realistically improve

Brightness, contrast and saturation adjustments can make an underexposed or flat photograph easier to read. Grayscale and black-and-white modes can also help when colour is distracting or a document needs a monochrome appearance. These operations are different from AI restoration: they change captured pixels rather than inventing missing detail.

A sensible enhancement workflow

  1. Keep the original image.
  2. Start with Auto Enhance.
  3. Adjust brightness and contrast in small steps.
  4. Change saturation only when colour needs correction.
  5. Use grayscale or black-and-white when the destination requires it.
  6. Compare the preview with the original before downloading.

Why the original matters

Repeated JPEG exports can introduce additional compression. Keep the source file and create the enhanced copy only when you are satisfied with the settings.
