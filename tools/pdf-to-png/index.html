About PDF to PNG

PDF to PNG renders PDF pages into lossless PNG images. PNG is useful for text-heavy pages, diagrams, screenshots, and graphics where crisp edges matter more than the smaller files often produced by JPEG.

Good practice: Before creating the final pdf to png result, keep the original source available and decide what the output must achieve. A quick review of page order, dimensions, readability, or calculation inputs can prevent a technically correct file from being unsuitable for its actual purpose.

Before you finish: Treat the preview as a review stage, not just a download step. Check the parts that matter most for pdf to png, then open the exported file or result once more before sharing, printing, submitting, or replacing the original.

How to use PDF to PNG

  1. Upload the PDF
  2. select the required pages if supported
  3. choose the available scale or quality
  4. convert the pages
  5. inspect the PNGs
  6. download the outputs.

Technical limitations

  • PNG files can become large at high resolution.
  • Very large or image-heavy PDFs can use substantial browser memory.
  • The output is a flat image and does not retain PDF links, forms, or selectable text.
  • Resolution affects both clarity and file size.

Frequently asked

Why choose PNG instead of JPG?

PNG is lossless and can keep text and sharp graphics cleaner, although files may be larger.

Can I convert a single page?

Yes, when the page-selection control is available.

Will the PNG be searchable?

No. It is an image of the page.

Why does a high-resolution PNG take longer?

More pixels have to be rendered and encoded in the browser.
