About Advanced Grammar Checker

This browser-based Advanced Grammar Checker checks a broad set of English grammar patterns, including all 12 traditional tense forms, subject–verb agreement, passive voice, conditionals, articles and determiners, prepositions and verb collocations, conjunctions, gerunds and infinitives, pronouns, common word confusions, punctuation, spacing, sentence structure and a growing spelling dictionary. Problems are highlighted directly in the text and listed below with an explanation and suggested correction. It is a rule-based browser checker, so context-dependent meaning and every possible English construction cannot be proven automatically.

For important writing, treat the suggestions as review prompts. The checker does not understand every sentence, context, name, technical term or style choice, so a human final review is still necessary.

Grammar coverage

The checker is designed as a broad first-pass English grammar review. Its tense rules cover simple present, present continuous, present perfect, present perfect continuous, simple past, past continuous, past perfect, past perfect continuous, simple future, future continuous, future perfect and future perfect continuous patterns. It also checks subject–verb agreement, passive voice, first/second/third conditional patterns, articles and determiners, conjunctions, preposition and verb combinations, gerund/infinitive complements, pronouns, punctuation, capitalization, comma splices, fragments, run-ons, parallelism, word order, spelling, US/UK spelling consistency, redundancy, readability and selected active-voice opportunities. It also makes a conservative Academic, Professional/Business, Casual or Creative tone detection and reports that tone with the results.

How to use Advanced Grammar Checker

  1. Paste or type English text into the editor.
  2. Select Check grammar to scan for supported grammar, tense, structure, spelling and punctuation patterns.
  3. Read each suggestion in context instead of accepting every rule automatically.
  4. Edit the text yourself, then run the checker again to confirm that the obvious issues are resolved.
  5. Use Copy current text after your final review.

Technical limitations

  • English only; Hindi and other languages are not comprehensively checked.
  • The checker uses deterministic rules and does not provide a complete dictionary or semantic proof of correctness.
  • Technical names, brand names, abbreviations and deliberate style choices can trigger suggestions that should be ignored.
  • The checker covers many common grammar and tense patterns, but English is context-sensitive; unusual, literary, technical or deliberately styled sentences may need human review.
  • Tense selection can depend on meaning and surrounding sentences, so the checker flags suspicious forms rather than pretending every tense choice has one automatic answer.

Frequently asked

Does the Grammar Checker understand every sentence?

No. It checks a defined set of common patterns. It is a review aid, not a complete language editor.

Can I use it for Hindi grammar?

Not reliably. The current checker is designed for English text and does not claim comprehensive Hindi grammar support.

Will every suggestion be correct?

No. Context matters. Review suggestions before changing names, technical terms, quoted text or intentional stylistic choices.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

The checker runs in the browser and does not intentionally send the text to a DSUTILITY server.
