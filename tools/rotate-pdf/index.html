Rotate PDF Pages

Rotate all pages or just the ones you select — 90°, 180°, or 270°. Quality is preserved perfectly.

🔒 Processed locally in your browser.

Drop a PDF here or click to select

One file at a time.

Click page thumbnails to select them, then choose a rotation.

How to rotate a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above.
  2. Wait while page thumbnails load.
  3. Click the thumbnails you want to rotate (or Select all).
  4. Choose 90° left, 90° right, or 180°.
  5. Click Apply & Download to save the rotated PDF.

Technical limitations

  • Rotating modifies the view rotation metadata — the same way Adobe Reader's rotate buttons work. Content is untouched, so quality is 100% preserved.
  • Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first (use our Unlock PDF tool).
  • Individual page rotations are supported — mixed orientations in one document are fine.

Frequently asked

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. Only the page's rotation metadata changes. The actual content is not re-rendered or re-compressed.

Can I rotate only specific pages?

Yes — click the thumbnails you want, then pick a rotation. Unselected pages stay as they were.

What about scanned PDFs?

Same behavior. Rotation is a metadata change, not a re-scan.
