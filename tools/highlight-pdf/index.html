About Highlight PDF

Highlight PDF adds visible emphasis to important areas of a document. It is useful for reviewing clauses, marking study material, identifying figures, or preparing a document for discussion without rewriting the source text.

Good practice: Before creating the final highlight pdf result, keep the original source available and decide what the output must achieve. A quick review of page order, dimensions, readability, or calculation inputs can prevent a technically correct file from being unsuitable for its actual purpose.

Before you finish: Treat the preview as a review stage, not just a download step. Check the parts that matter most for highlight pdf, then open the exported file or result once more before sharing, printing, submitting, or replacing the original.

How to use Highlight PDF

  1. Upload the PDF
  2. select the highlight tool
  3. drag across the target text or area
  4. adjust color, opacity, or thickness when available
  5. review overlapping marks
  6. save the annotated PDF.

Technical limitations

  • Highlighting is visual annotation and does not change the underlying source text.
  • Scanned pages may require different handling because there is no selectable text layer.
  • Too many opaque highlights can reduce readability.
  • Modifying a signed PDF can invalidate its digital signature.

Frequently asked

Can I highlight scanned pages?

Visual marking can still be useful, but text-selection behavior depends on the source PDF.

Can I change highlight color?

Use the available color control to distinguish different review categories.

Does highlighting edit the text?

No. It adds a visual mark over the page.

Can I remove a highlight later?

Keep the editable/source PDF or use the editor’s element controls before exporting when available.
