Background Remover

Remove a plain or fairly uniform photo background locally and download a transparent PNG.

Choose an image.

Honest limitation

This lightweight client-side remover is designed for plain or fairly uniform backgrounds. It is not an AI portrait-segmentation model, so hair, shadows and complex backgrounds may need another workflow.

Background Remover Guide

How the browser-based removal works

The tool samples colours from the image edges and follows connected pixels that are close to those edge colours. Pixels classified as background become transparent while the central subject is retained. The tolerance slider controls how broadly similar edge colours are treated as background.

When this method works well

It is most useful for portraits, signatures, product shots and scanned images photographed against a fairly even background. It is less reliable when the subject touches the edge, the background contains strong patterns, or the subject and background have very similar colours.

Before you download

  1. Choose a high-resolution image with a clear background boundary.
  2. Start with a low tolerance and increase it gradually.
  3. Inspect hair, clothing edges and fine details at the preview.
  4. If the subject is damaged, lower the tolerance or use a different source image.
  5. Download PNG when you need transparency.

Privacy and limitations

The image is processed in the browser. This lightweight method is not a replacement for a machine-learning segmentation model, so it should not be described as professional studio background extraction.
