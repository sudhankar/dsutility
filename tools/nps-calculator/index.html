NPS Calculator

Estimate NPS contributions and a projected retirement corpus from your salary, contribution rates, investment period and assumed annual return.

🔒 Calculated locally in your browser.

What this calculator does

This calculator gives a mathematical estimate of NPS contributions and a possible future corpus. It combines the employee and employer contribution percentages you enter, applies them to the monthly Basic Pay + DA, and compounds the contribution stream using your assumed annual return. You can also model a simple annual salary-growth assumption.

Central Government contribution context

For Central Government employees covered by NPS, PFRDA currently describes a monthly employee contribution of 10% of salary (Basic plus DA) and a Government contribution of 14% of salary (Basic plus DA). These defaults are prefilled only as a starting point. They are not universal: State Government and other sector rules can differ, so change the inputs when your applicable rules are different.

How to use it

  1. Enter the monthly Basic Pay plus DA that you want to use as the starting salary base.
  2. Enter the number of years you expect contributions to continue.
  3. Check or change the employee and employer contribution percentages.
  4. Enter an assumed annual return. This is an assumption, not a promised NPS return.
  5. Optionally enter annual salary growth to model a gradually increasing contribution base.
  6. Click Calculate NPS Estimate and review the contribution and projected corpus figures.

Limitations and honest expectations

  • This is an estimate, not an NPS account statement or a retirement benefit guarantee.
  • Investment returns vary and are not fixed by this calculator.
  • The calculation does not model every NPS investment choice, charges, taxation, exit/withdrawal rules, annuity purchase, partial withdrawals or future regulatory changes.
  • Salary growth is represented as a simple annual percentage; real pay changes can occur at different dates.
  • For State Government employees, Autonomous Bodies and other sectors, applicable contribution rules may differ.

Privacy

Your salary and assumptions are processed in your browser for this calculation. The tool does not need to upload those figures to a DSUTILITY processing server.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator use the current Central Government NPS rates?

The default inputs are 10% employee and 14% employer contribution because PFRDA describes those rates for Central Government employees. You should verify the rules applicable to your own service or State before relying on the result.

Can I use it for a State Government employee?

Yes, as a mathematical estimator, but you should change the contribution inputs to match the applicable State rules and verify them from the relevant notification.

Does the result show the pension I will receive?

No. It estimates the accumulated corpus under the assumptions you enter. Actual retirement benefits depend on NPS rules, investment performance and the applicable exit and annuity arrangements.
