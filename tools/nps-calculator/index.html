NPS Calculator

Estimate NPS contributions and a projected retirement corpus from your salary, contribution rates, investment period and assumed annual return.

🔒 Calculated locally in your browser.

About NPS Calculator

NPS Calculator estimates retirement-related outcomes using the contribution, return, tenure, and withdrawal assumptions entered by the user. It is intended for planning and comparison, not as an official account statement.

Good practice: Before creating the final nps calculator result, keep the original source available and decide what the output must achieve. Before you finish: Treat the preview as a review stage, not just a download step. Check the parts that matter most for nps calculator, then open the exported file or result once more before sharing, printing, submitting, or replacing the original.

How to use NPS Calculator

  1. Enter the contribution or salary-related inputs
  2. choose the investment/return assumptions
  3. set the investment period or retirement age
  4. calculate
  5. review the accumulated value and other displayed figures
  6. change one assumption at a time to compare scenarios.

Technical limitations

  • Actual NPS outcomes depend on market returns, charges, contribution history, and scheme rules.
  • The calculator uses assumptions rather than live NPS account data.
  • Tax, annuity, and withdrawal rules can change and should be verified separately.
  • A projection should not be treated as a guaranteed return.

Frequently asked

Does it show my real NPS balance?

No. It calculates a projection from the inputs you provide.

Can I change the expected return?

Use the available assumption controls to test different scenarios.

Is the result guaranteed?

No. Market-linked returns and future rules are uncertain.

Should I use it for an official claim?

No. Verify official figures against your NPS statement and applicable rules.

Privacy

NPS calculations are performed locally from the values entered into the calculator. The tool does not connect to an individual NPS account or retrieve live contribution records. Treat the displayed figures as estimates and protect any personal financial inputs on the device you use.
