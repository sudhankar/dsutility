How this GPF calculator works

The visible statement includes Month, Subscription, Refund, two DA Arrear entries, Bonus, Other, Total Deposit, Withdrawal, applicable Interest Rate, Monthly Interest and Closing Balance. The calculation uses the statement entries internally while keeping the working calculation columns out of the visible statement.

For the supplied sample, the annual result is reproduced by calculating each month's interest from the opening balance and the running contribution balance. For a constant rate, this gives the same annual interest result; a rate change is applied from its effective month. The sample also shows the closing balance after adding deposits and interest and subtracting withdrawal.

Interest rate changes during the year

If the rate changes in the middle of the selected period, use Add Rate Change, select the effective month and enter the new annual rate. The table then shows the applicable rate for each month, avoiding a single rate being incorrectly applied to the entire period.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter two DA arrears in the same year?

Yes. The statement has separate DA Arrear 1 and DA Arrear 2 columns so two separate entries can be recorded.

Can I enter bonus and other deposits?

Yes. Bonus and Other are separate editable columns and are included in the monthly deposit and progressive calculation.
