About FD Calculator

FD Calculator estimates fixed-deposit maturity and interest from the principal, rate, tenure, and compounding assumptions entered by the user. It is useful for comparing deposit scenarios and understanding the relationship between principal, interest, and maturity value.

How to use FD Calculator

  1. Enter the deposit amount
  2. enter the interest rate
  3. choose the tenure and compounding option
  4. calculate
  5. review principal, estimated interest, and maturity value
  6. compare another rate or tenure if needed.

Technical limitations

  • Banks can use different compounding conventions, payout schedules, and rate rules.
  • Actual maturity can differ when tax, premature withdrawal, or special customer rates apply.
  • The calculator is not connected to a bank account or current bank rate feed.
  • Use the bank’s official terms for a final maturity figure.

Frequently asked

What is interest earned?

It is the estimated interest generated by the entered deposit under the selected calculation method.

Can I compare different tenures?

Yes. Change the tenure and compare the resulting maturity value.

Does it include tax deducted at source?

Only if the calculator explicitly provides such an input; otherwise check tax separately.

Is the result guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate based on the rate and compounding assumptions entered.

Privacy

The FD calculation is performed locally in the browser. The tool does not retrieve your bank account information or submit the entered figures to a bank. Treat rates as user-supplied assumptions and verify the final maturity against the bank’s official schedule.
