Fixed-deposit maturity estimate

This calculator models a cumulative fixed deposit using principal, annual interest rate and tenure. The default model uses quarterly compounding.

Formula

M = P × (1 + r/n)^(n × t)

P is principal, r is annual rate in decimal form, n is four quarterly periods per year and t is tenure.

What to verify

  • Cumulative or periodic interest payout.
  • Actual compounding frequency.
  • Premature-closure rules.
  • Applicable tax or TDS treatment.
  • Special rates and product terms.

FAQ

Is this an official bank calculator?

No. It is a general mathematical estimate.

Why might a bank statement differ?

Product rules, payout timing, taxes and rounding can change the actual amount.
