Leave Encashment Calculator

Estimate earned-leave encashment from Basic Pay, DA and the number of eligible leave days you enter.

🔒 Calculated locally in your browser.

What this calculator does

This calculator gives a simple estimate of earned-leave cash equivalent by using the monthly Basic Pay plus the DA percentage you enter, dividing the resulting monthly leave salary base by 30, and multiplying it by the eligible leave days. It is intended as a checking aid, not an official sanction or retirement-benefit statement.

Central Government context

Central Government leave-encashment rules have provisions for cash equivalent of leave salary and a ceiling of 300 days in relevant circumstances. DoPT material also explains the treatment of earned leave and half-pay leave in specified encashment cases. This calculator focuses on the simpler earned-leave estimate and does not attempt to model every CCS (Leave) Rules scenario.

How to use it

  1. Enter the Basic Pay applicable to the calculation.
  2. Enter the DA percentage applicable to the leave salary calculation.
  3. Enter the number of eligible earned-leave days.
  4. Keep the 300-day ceiling enabled when it matches the rules applicable to your case.
  5. Click Calculate Estimate and review the monthly base, daily equivalent and estimated amount.

Limitations and honest expectations

  • This is a mathematical estimate, not an official government calculation.
  • It does not automatically determine whether every entered day is actually eligible for encashment.
  • It focuses on earned leave and does not calculate every half-pay-leave, LTC, absorption, resignation, death or special-service-rule scenario.
  • State Government, PSU, autonomous-body and other service rules may differ.
  • Verify the applicable rule, pay components, ceiling and office order before using the amount in a claim or retirement file.

Privacy

The figures you enter are processed locally in your browser. The calculator does not need to upload your salary or leave information to a DSUTILITY server.

Frequently asked questions

Does it calculate half-pay leave encashment?

No. This version focuses on a straightforward earned-leave estimate so that the formula remains transparent. More complex leave-rule scenarios should be checked separately.

Why is the 300-day option editable?

Because not every employee category follows the same service rules. The 300-day ceiling is presented as a Central Government context, not as a universal rule.

Is the result official?

No. Use it as an independent estimate and verify the applicable service rules and records before relying on the amount officially.
