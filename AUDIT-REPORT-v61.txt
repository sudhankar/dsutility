------------------------

SWP:
- "Total withdrawal" label is explicit.
- Starting corpus is exposed as "Total invested".
- Withdrawal is accumulated from the simulated monthly withdrawals.
- Effective monthly growth is applied before the scheduled withdrawal.

FD:
- Maturity, interest earned and principal outputs are calculated.

RD:
- Total invested = monthly contribution × number of months.
- Estimated interest = maturity − total invested.
- Monthly instalments are treated separately using the quarterly rate over each instalment's remaining time.
